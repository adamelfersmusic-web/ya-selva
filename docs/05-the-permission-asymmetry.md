# The Permission Asymmetry

```
BREAK THIS LOOP  →  requires PERMISSION.  Collaborators, coordination, waiting.
YA SELVA         →  requires NOTHING.     Bag, Push, guitar, mic. Go.
```

Ya Selva launched the day Break This Loop felt like "constantly waiting for other people's
permission." That frustration produced the decision: make my own project.

**This is why BTL went dim.** Not scope, not money, not discipline. Every step forward
required someone else to say yes — and a project gated on other people cannot be driven by
a person whose gift is momentum.

## Ya Selva came first

Before Break This Loop existed there was a vision board for essentially this exact project:

```
laptop · Ableton Push · acoustic guitar · mic
small enough to fit in a bag
perform anywhere · collaborate with anyone, anywhere
```

The order is: **the dream, the escalation, the return.** Ya Selva isn't the scaled-down
consolation. It's the thing that came first.

## The open license makes cross-promotion structural

Everyone who joins BTL agrees any participant may collaborate with their material. That gives
Ya Selva a permanent, permission-free supply of collaborators. Split-screen with someone in
India tonight — the consent already happened at upload.

## Fungibility

```
any BTL idea that stalls     →  collapse it INTO Ya Selva at one-life scale
any Ya Selva idea that works →  export it TO Break This Loop
```

Nothing is stranded.

## The endgame — founder as ambassador

```
NOW     founder AND operator AND artist   ← unsustainable
LATER   BREAK THIS LOOP  →  a team. Vision clear enough to run without him.
        YA SELVA         →  the founder, out front, being himself.
                            Him being himself IS the promotion.
```

Reference: Gil 'the Grid' Gomes Leal and AMENTI. The moniker exists because he connects
worlds and people — his artist identity *is* the connective function.

This resolves the worry about centering yourself. Ya Selva isn't centering a person —
it's living the culture in public.

## Is it solved?

The design is. Two things aren't, and neither can be solved by thinking:

1. **Energy.** Workshop plus concert, one night, one person who can do both halves.
2. **Whether five cities stack** as well as one room did.

**The risk changed shape: from UNCLEAR to UNSTARTED.** The only remaining move is a date.
