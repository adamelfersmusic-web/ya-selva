# The Pitch — credential, demonstration, reach-out

## Two credibility assets, not equal

```
CREDENTIAL     "I founded, organized and executed a global collaboration
                across 20+ countries and six continents"
               → past tense · scale · must be BELIEVED

DEMONSTRATION  30 strangers singing, tonight, in this room
               → present tense · mechanism · impossible to FAKE
```

In a community whose entire practice is embodiment and presence, the demonstration
massively outranks the resume. Anyone can claim twenty countries. Nobody can fake
getting a room to open up.

## The frame

**THE FOUNDER IS ALSO THE SMALLEST WORKING VERSION OF THE PROJECT.**

Break This Loop says anyone, anywhere, can add their piece. Ya Selva is not the guy who
describes that — he is the guy walking around being it, with a bag. Break This Loop in a bag.
The scale is the proof of concept; he is the proof of mechanism.

This is also the antidote to being seen as infrastructure. A founder with only the resume
reads as an administrator. A founder who can activate any room reads as the real thing.

## The ask is an invitation

```
NOT   "can I play your room"
BUT   "your community can be in a thing that already spans 20+ countries
       and six continents — and you would be the one who brought it to them"
```

The host is not doing you a favour. You are handing them something they get to give
their people. That is status for them, in their own room.

## The demo is free and portable

```
WITHIN DRIVING DISTANCE  →  do not send the message. SHOW UP. Run twenty minutes.
TOO FAR TO DRIVE         →  trailer + message
```

Sort the map by drive time, not just warmth.

## The trailer

~90 seconds of existing global footage cut to the song. A weekend, not a project.
With it, the message barely has to work. Make it before sending anything.

## Copy rules

- **Understate.** No adjectives on the numbers.
- **Credential early, then move on.** One sentence.
- **Keep "I do not want to fly in and run someone else room."** It is the line that closes.
- **End with a call, not a booking.** The call is where the demonstration happens.

## Sequence

```
1. MAKE THE MAP        recall only, low energy
2. CUT THE TRAILER     ~90 sec, existing footage
3. Talk to Ayman       parallel
4. Send ONE message    to the warmest name on the map
```
