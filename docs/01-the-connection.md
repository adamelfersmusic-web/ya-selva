# Ya Selva → Break This Loop — the connection

Canonical. 2026-08-07.

## The four named things

| | what it is | belongs to | presence |
|---|---|---|---|
| BREAK THIS LOOP | the movement, the platform, the archive | everyone | runs without him |
| MOONLIGHT | the gathering — a BTL night, in a city | BTL | needs him in the room |
| UNDERPASS | the secret pop-up, location undisclosed | Ya Selva | needs him in the room |
| YA SELVA | the artist project, the character | him | needs him on stage |
| THE VILLAGE | his audience — the list, the drops | him | — |

## The definition — presence, not aesthetics

```
MOONLIGHT   needs you in the room
YA SELVA    needs you on stage
BTL         runs without you — that IS its definition
```

The schism was never earthy vs urban. It was presence-dependent vs presence-independent.

## The relationship — participant, not component

Ya Selva is not BTL's host, owner, or aesthetic. He is its first and most visible
participant — the thesis performed by one person.

| BTL claims | Ya Selva demonstrates |
|---|---|
| anyone, anywhere, can add their piece | a guy who shows up anywhere and makes something |
| strangers become collaborators | ten people gather under a bridge and sing |
| found sound, any culture, any medium | street lamps as percussion, field samples, live loops |
| it travels | he wanders |

Every Ya Selva video demonstrates BTL's premise without being a BTL product.
A participant is not a brand. A participant is proof.

Ya Selva is a person. Break This Loop is a place. An artist has a sound; a platform
hosts many. Burning Man does not sound like its founders.

## The loop

```
YA SELVA collects      →  the archive
YA SELVA sings         →  his songs, made from what he found
BREAK THIS LOOP        →  what happens when everyone else
                          gets to do the same thing with the same material
```

Graceland is the template. Travel, record local musicians in their own idiom, then
make your own songs from it. Not a field-recording album, not a solo album — both.

Caveat: Graceland is also the most-argued-about extraction case. Credit, consent and
compensation from day one is what separates the record people love from the one people
litigate.

## Two kinds of capture, one night

| capture type | where | usable as BTL stems? |
|---|---|---|
| isolated contribution — one person, clean A/V, on the grid | the breakout rooms | YES — this is the Lego |
| room capture — crowd singing, the jam, the set | circle and show | NO — but it is the footage, proof, marketing |

The breakout rooms are the isolation booth, disguised as an experience.

## The rules

1. The night is the atom.
2. Never cut the performance.
3. Capture and turnaround are somebody else's job.
4. Use the warm start; never let one scene be the only channel.
5. Seeding is legitimate; pretending it's live is not.
6. Credit, consent, compensation from day one.
7. Historic accumulates. It doesn't arrive.
