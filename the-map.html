The terrain

This community already exists. It isn't a market to build — it's a network that's already there, that Ya Selva is already part of, and that can be mapped from relationships you already have.

Song CirclesEcstatic DanceDrum CirclesCacao MusiciansProducersDancersRetreats Conscious CommunityCo-OpsOther

These are not ten separate scenes — they're one overlapping population. The person running the cacao ceremony knows the ecstatic-dance host, who books the drum-circle facilitator, who lives at the co-op. Map one city and a few calls give you most of it. The map is a graph, not a list — which is what makes it tourable.

CONTRIBUTORS  Musicians · Producers · Dancers
              → they run breakout rooms, or fill them

⚠ Producers are the constraint, not hosts. You need 3–4 per city who can run a room and record cleanly. Hosts you have in abundance. A city with a great host and no producers can run an Underpass — not a Moonlight.

Co-Ops are the sleeper — the only entry that's a building rather than a person. Multiple rooms, often cheap or free, resident community.  ·  Cacao is the freshness indicator — newest of these practices, so it maps where things are currently active.

The method

1. Brain-dump. No structure. Every name that surfaces. DON'T FILTER.
2. Go city by city — geography jogs memory better than category.
3. Scan Instagram following, old event pages, festival lineups, tagged photos.
   Most of the map is already in your phone.
4. Then fill the columns. Then read the tiers.

Filtering while dumping is what turns a 20-minute task into a three-week one that never finishes.
