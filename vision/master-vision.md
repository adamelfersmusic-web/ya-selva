# YA SELVA — MASTER VISION

> **A moving musical center small enough to travel anywhere, porous enough to become larger through every place it touches.**

Ya Selva carries the spine:
- the songs
- the rig
- the sonic language
- the participatory method
- the identity

The world supplies the body:
- musicians
- dancers
- voices
- rhythms
- rooms
- streets
- field sounds
- local energy

> **Same sound. Different body.**

Every city should not get the same concert.
The core remains recognizable and world-class, but each place changes the body of the show.

Ya Selva is the moving human center.

Break This Loop is the participation architecture that lets the world answer, contribute, remix, and carry the signal farther.

> **Ya Selva encounters the world.**
> **Break This Loop lets the world answer.**

The bridge between them is the distributed ensemble:

> **Ya Selva carries the spine. The ensemble lives in the world.**

Over time, trusted collaborators accumulate across cities. They are not random local hires. They are recurring relationships who know the work, have touched the music, and can reactivate when Ya Selva returns.

The tour therefore becomes part of the composition.
Songs mutate through geography, people, repeated encounters, and return visits. The archive becomes evidence of how the work changed because it actually moved through the world.

The tiny rig is what makes the whole thing physically possible.

> **Do not design a portable version of a big show.**
> **Design a tiny complete show that the world can expand.**

The long game is not merely a tour, a band, or a collaboration platform.

It is a **distributed living ensemble**, a musical archive of relationships, and a body of work that becomes impossible to separate from the life that created it.

## Governing lines

> **Find the pulse. Follow it. Make something real. Leave the signal.**

> **Same sound. Different body.**

> **Ya Selva carries the spine. The ensemble lives in the world.**

> **Ya Selva encounters the world. Break This Loop lets the world answer.**

> **The world is not the backdrop for the music. The world is one of the musicians.**
