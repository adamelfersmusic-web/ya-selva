# UNDERPASS — the secret show

`UNDERPASS 001 — location undisclosed`

## The mechanic

```
Announce a show — location undisclosed
      ↓
Only THE VILLAGE list gets the location
      ↓
Footage appears online. Nobody can tell where it was.
      ↓
People who missed it want in
      ↓
They join the list to find out next time
      ↓
They attend → they become the footage
```

The conversion event is joining the list, not buying a ticket.

## Why it inverts marketing

Standard growth is PUSH — announce, promote, chase. This is PULL: the withholding
is the promotion. People who miss one feel FOMO and want the next invite, so they
come to you.

## Why it reads authentic

The secrecy is operationally REAL. You cannot publicly announce a show under a
bridge — permits, police, capacity, the space. Manufactured scarcity reads as a
tactic; a show that genuinely cannot be announced reads as a secret.

## Two formats, different jobs

| | MOONLIGHT | UNDERPASS |
|---|---|---|
| job | production engine | marketing engine |
| structure | full ritual — circle, breakouts, closing, jam, set | a show that appears and disappears |
| access | ticketed / announced | list-only, undisclosed |
| output | contributions for the archive | footage, mystique, list growth |

## The ceiling

It works until it works too well. Cannot be booked, sold, or scaled — top of funnel,
never the business. When 300 people show up to a bridge the format breaks. Cap the
list per event, rotate cities, or graduate to a venue with a smaller secret series
continuing underneath.
