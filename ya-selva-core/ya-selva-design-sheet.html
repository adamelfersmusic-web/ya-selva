Inside a breakout room

1. producer plays the bed at tempo + key, drone under it     0:30
2. contributor hears it once through headphones               0:30
3. take 1 — 8 or 16 bars                                      0:30
4. takes 2 & 3 — different feel each time                     2:00
5. PLAY IT BACK TO THEM. they hear themselves in the song.    1:00
6. name, contact, consent. reset.                             1:00
                                                    ≈ 6–10 min

The hook drop PROVEN — PERENNIAL · SISTER STONE · THANE OF EARTH

beat cuts → rim click on 2 & 4 → drone underneath
click in one ear, one out to hear the room
30+ people at full volume · 3 takes × 16 bars
omni rolling the whole time — never stop between takes

PROVEN AT 20 PEOPLE: one AirPod, hands, foot stomp. synced perfectly.
THE STOMP = the conductor AND the slate. one motion, two jobs.
gotchafix
click fixes tempo, NOT pitchquiet drone under it holding the root
phone clocks driftone recorder = clock master · short takes · slate
—hook must be learnable by strangers in 60 seconds

Instrument doctrine — treat as law

PERCUSSION → hand out freely.  forgiving. bad player still in the pocket.
MELODIC    → never hand out.   one untrained horn wrecks a room in 8 bars.
VOICE      → the universal.    nobody can bring it badly.
Pass out at ONE point in the setnever at the door
Pre-select the melodic playersreal chops only, per town
Selected players stand IN the crowdsound from every direction
Stage in the CENTREeverything radiates

Capture

Producer protocol — same in every room

new audio track  →  NAME IT WITH THEIR NAME   ← this is the credit list
arm it           →  three takes on three tracks
keep the best    →  mute the others
END              →  edit nothing. bounce the room to one stereo file.

ASSEMBLY: 4 stereo bounces onto the bed → rough levels → play.
the stacking already happened, in parallel, during the night.

Growth

UNDERPASS — inverted marketing

announce a show — LOCATION UNDISCLOSED
      ↓
only THE VILLAGE list gets the location
      ↓
footage appears online. nobody can tell where it was.
      ↓
people who missed it want in
      ↓
they join the list to find out next time
      ↓
they attend → they become the footage

CONVERSION EVENT = joining the list, not buying a ticket.
the secrecy is OPERATIONALLY REAL — you can't announce a bridge show.

The host model

LOCAL HOST   runs THE GATHERING — the whole front half
ADAM         runs the breakout rooms, then plays

booking a venue = a visiting act.
handing over the front half = a collaborator.
"I trust you with my room."

THE TRUST IS THE ASSET. they're not lending a venue —
they're lending CREDIBILITY. guarded rooms produce guarded takes.

The seedling

NIGHT 1   they lend credibility, run the Gathering
          → their people are in the release, tagged, by name
NIGHT 2   they know the format. seen it work on their own community.
NIGHT 3   they're asking when the next one is. they have opinions.
          → they could run the front half without you in the building

nobody recruited them. they learned it by doing it.
THE HOSTS ARE THE YES — and they're saying it to their own people.

Rules & Decisions

The seven rules

1  the night is the atom. everything else is downstream of one night.
2  never cut the performance. without the set it's a filming session.
3  capture + turnaround are SOMEBODY ELSE'S JOB.
4  use the warm start. never let one scene be the only channel.
5  seeding is legitimate. pretending it's arriving live is not.
6  credit, consent, compensation from day one.
7  historic accumulates. it doesn't arrive.
